# Azure Backup Jobs

## What are Backup Jobs?

Backup Jobs show the status of backup and restore operations.

---

## Location

Recovery Services Vault
→ Monitoring
→ Backup Jobs

---

## Status Examples

- In Progress
- Completed
- Failed
- Pending

---

## Purpose

Used for:
- Monitoring
- Troubleshooting
- Verifying backup success

---

## Important Learning

Large VMs may take:

- 30 minutes
- 45 minutes
- Or longer

depending on:
- Data size
- Running applications
- Workload
